Under the hood

Algorithmic, not a model

Softwave is not a neural clone of a hosted music service. It writes music with a seeded random generator, music theory and a small Web Audio synth kit. That keeps it instant, free and reproducible.

01

Seeded

Your prompt and seed drive a mulberry32 PRNG. The same inputs always produce the same notes, so a track can be rebuilt or shared.

02

Composed

Mood presets pick progressions, voicings, motifs and drum patterns. Sections build from intro to peak and back down again.

03

Played

A lookahead scheduler drives subtractive synths, noise drums, reverb and delay. The offline renderer reuses the same graph for export.
